Our Story

38 Years of Weaving
Indian Heritage

Founded in 1985 in the heart of India, Balaji Cloth House began as a humble store with a single dream — to make authentic, handcrafted Indian sarees accessible to every woman. Today, we are proud custodians of a living tradition.

We work directly with master weavers across Banaras, Kanchipuram, Chanderi, and Sambhalpur, ensuring that every saree carries the authentic touch of a craftsman's hands — and a fair livelihood for their families.

Our Heritage

The Story of Balaji Cloth House

Since 1985

A Legacy Built on Thread & Trust

In 1985, Shri Balaji Prasad Sharma opened a small saree shop in Varanasi with a simple belief: that every Indian woman deserves to wear something truly beautiful without compromise. What started as a modest store with a few Banarasi silk sarees has grown into one of India's most trusted names in ethnic wear.

For nearly four decades, we have built relationships — not just with customers, but with the artisans who create these masterpieces. We travel to weaving clusters in Varanasi, Kanchipuram, Chanderi, Sambhalpur, and Pochampally to source directly from the hands that create them.

Today, Balaji Cloth House serves over 50,000 women across India through our physical stores and online platform, with a catalogue spanning 5,000+ unique designs across silk, cotton, georgette, and more.

Artisan Support

We pay 30% above market rate to our weavers, supporting over 200 artisan families.

Eco-Conscious

Natural dyes and sustainable packaging — because the earth deserves respect too.

GI Certified

We only sell products with Geographical Indication tags where applicable.
